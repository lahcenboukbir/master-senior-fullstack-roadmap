# Who I Am & What I Need

## Background
- Junior fullstack web developer.
- Current stack: PHP, Laravel, MySQL, React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS.
- Comfortable shipping features with this stack, but admits to gaps in the fundamentals underneath it — uses many tools/patterns without fully knowing *why*, *how*, or *when* they apply.

## Goal
Become a **senior fullstack developer** — not just someone who writes code that works, but someone who understands systems deeply, thinks from first principles, and can debug/reason about problems, not just follow patterns.

Explicitly **not** just "a coder" — wants full understanding from scratch: computer fundamentals, language internals, data structures, databases, architecture, security, and the senior mindset (tradeoffs, communication, problem-solving), not only framework APIs.

## Stack Sequencing (deliberate, not accidental)
1. **JS fullstack first** (React, Next.js, Node.js, Express, NestJS, JavaScript, TypeScript) — used for freelancing, personal projects, and startup work. This is the near-term, hands-on track.
2. **Java + Spring Boot last** — deliberately deferred to the end, specifically to target larger/enterprise job-market opportunities once the JS track is solid.

## Learning Constraints & Preferences
- **No deadline.** Studying ~2 hours/day, indefinitely — optimize for depth and real understanding, not speed-to-employable.
- Because there's no time pressure, **fundamentals should default to "Master" tier**, not be trimmed down to "Know Only" just because they're not used daily on the job. Only genuinely advanced/specialist/adjacent-career topics (e.g., Kubernetes, Terraform, distributed consensus, DDD strategic design, FinOps, chaos engineering, mobile-specific work) should stay at Know Only / Skip.
- Prefers plain, direct language over jargon-heavy questions — if a question is unclear, simplify it rather than assume.
- Wants to be walked through *why* something works, not just told the steps.
